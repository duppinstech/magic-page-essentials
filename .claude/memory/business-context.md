## Product and positioning
- **Product:** Magic Page Essentials, by Duppins Technology (https://duppinstech.com).
- **Audience:** Sites that already use the third-party Magic Page plugin and need a usable Pages list plus a record of how those SEO pages perform.
- **Positioning:** A Duppins Technology add-on for Magic Page. It keeps wp-admin usable and records views, clicks, and 404s. It does not replace Magic Page, and Duppins Technology does not make Magic Page.
- **Built for (client):** product work
